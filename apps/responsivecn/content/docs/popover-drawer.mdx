title: PopoverDrawer
description: An anchored Popover at desktop that becomes a swipeable Drawer below 768px.

A drop-in replacement for the shadcn [Popover](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/popover): at 768px and up it renders your Popover unchanged; below, the same children render inside your [Drawer](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs/components/drawer). The [breakpoint](/docs/breakpoint) and the [override prop](/docs/override-prop) work the same for every pair, so this page covers only what is specific to this one.

## Migrating

Change the imports and insert `Drawer` after `Popover` in each part name: `PopoverContent` becomes `PopoverDrawerContent`, and so on, with the same props and children (see [Migrating](/docs/installation#migrating)). Then check two things below 768px: the [desktop-only props](#desktop-only-props), and a title on every content (see [Accessibility](#accessibility)).

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  PopoverDrawer,
  PopoverDrawerContent,
  PopoverDrawerDescription,
  PopoverDrawerHeader,
  PopoverDrawerTitle,
  PopoverDrawerTrigger,

```

```tsx
<PopoverDrawer>

  <PopoverDrawerContent>

      <PopoverDrawerDescription>Description</PopoverDrawerDescription>
    </PopoverDrawerHeader>
  </PopoverDrawerContent>
</PopoverDrawer>
```

## Composition

```txt
PopoverDrawer
├── PopoverDrawerAnchor (Radix, optional)
├── PopoverDrawerTrigger
└── PopoverDrawerContent
    ├── PopoverDrawerHeader
    │   ├── PopoverDrawerTitle
    │   └── PopoverDrawerDescription
    └── PopoverDrawerFooter (optional)
```

## The content box

Below 768px, `PopoverDrawerContent` wraps its children in a padded box with the Popover's column layout, so children written for the Popover render the same way in the Drawer. The `className` you pass to `PopoverDrawerContent` styles that box; `drawer={{ className }}` styles the drawer itself:

<ComponentDemo>
  <ComponentDemoRender>
    <PopoverDrawerContentBoxExample />
  </ComponentDemoRender>

```tsx
<PopoverDrawerContent className="w-80" drawer={{ className: "max-h-[80dvh]" }}>
```

  </ComponentDemoCode>

A width such as `w-80` therefore sizes the content, centered in a full-width drawer, instead of shrinking the drawer to a 320px panel. `PopoverDrawerHeader` and `PopoverDrawerFooter` take their spacing from that box, as they do in the Popover.

## The `modal` default

A Popover is non-modal by default; a Drawer is modal. With no `modal` prop, each side keeps its own default: an anchored popover that closes on an outside click at 768px and up, a drawer with an overlay below. Set `modal` to apply it to both sides, or `drawer={{ modal }}` to change only the drawer:

## The footer part

The shadcn Popover has no footer, but a drawer often needs an action row. `PopoverDrawerFooter` is optional: a `flex justify-end gap-2` row at 768px and up, your `DrawerFooter` below.

To close from a button inside the content, control `open`, as you would with the Popover:

<PopoverDrawer open={open} onOpenChange={setOpen}>

</PopoverDrawer>

## Desktop-only props

These props apply at 768px and up and are dropped below, because a drawer has no equivalent:

- **Positioning on the Content:** `side`, `align`, `sideOffset`, `alignOffset`, and on Radix the rest of the Popper props (`avoidCollisions`, `collisionPadding`, `sticky`, …).
- **Hover on the Trigger:** `openOnHover`, `delay`, `closeDelay`.
- **Detached triggers on Base UI:** `handle`, `triggerId` and `defaultTriggerId` on the root, `handle` and `payload` on the Trigger. Below 768px, the drawer opens only from a `PopoverDrawerTrigger` placed inside `PopoverDrawer`, or from a controlled `open`.
- **Anchoring on Radix:** `PopoverDrawerAnchor` renders your `PopoverAnchor` at 768px and up. Below 768px it renders its child (with `asChild`) or a plain `div`.

## Accessibility

Below 768px the content is a dialog, and a dialog needs a name. A popover without a title gets none: add a `PopoverDrawerTitle`, visually hidden if the design has no heading.

## API reference

Each part takes every prop of its shadcn Popover counterpart, plus a `drawer` prop that takes the props of the matching Drawer part, except `children`: `drawer` on the Content takes your `DrawerContent` props, and so on. Only the parts below leave out more.

### PopoverDrawer

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drawer` | `Omit<ComponentProps<typeof Drawer>, "children" \| RootState>` | - |

`RootState` stands for the keys that `drawer` leaves out because they belong to the shared root: `open` and `defaultOpen`, since both sides share one open state, and the detached-trigger props `handle`, `triggerId` and `defaultTriggerId`. `drawer.onOpenChange` runs after the shared `onOpenChange` and receives your Drawer's own arguments.

### PopoverDrawerTrigger

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `drawer` | `Omit<ComponentProps<typeof DrawerTrigger>, "children" \| "handle" \| "payload">` | - |

### PopoverDrawerFooter

Takes every prop of a `div`, plus `drawer` with your `DrawerFooter` props, except `children`.

### PopoverDrawerAnchor

Radix only. Takes every prop of your `PopoverAnchor` and adds none.

## Radix notes

- `drawer={{...}}` is typed on Vaul: `direction`, `dismissible`, `snapPoints`, and so on.
- Like Radix Popover, the drawer moves focus inside when it opens. Pass `drawer={{ autoFocus: false }}` to keep Vaul's own default.
- A non-modal Vaul drawer stays open when you tap outside it, unlike the non-modal Popover. Control `open` to close it from inside.
- Vaul, the drawer library under the Radix Drawer, is no longer maintained. It still works; the Base UI Drawer is the maintained option.
