---
title: Override prop
description: Pass mobile-only props through the drawer prop. className merges, state callbacks compose and every other prop replaces.
---

You tune the mobile side through the **override prop**, `drawer={{...}}`, on `DialogDrawer`, `SheetDrawer` and `PopoverDrawer`. It exists on the root **and on every part that has a mobile counterpart**, and is typed 1:1 on your installed Drawer, so you need no casts.

## Merge rules

- **`className` merges** as `cn(shared, mobile)`: the shared class still applies below the breakpoint, and the mobile one wins on conflicts. `PopoverDrawerContent` is the exception: its `className` styles [the content box](/docs/popover-drawer#the-content-box) inside the drawer.
- **A `className` function** receives the desktop component's state at 768px and up and your Drawer's state below.
- **State callbacks compose**: for `onOpenChange` and `onOpenChangeComplete` on the root, the shared handler runs first, then the mobile one with your Drawer's own arguments.
- **Every other prop replaces** the shared value below the breakpoint.

## Native defaults

Outside the override prop, each side keeps its native defaults. A shared prop is forwarded to both sides, except the desktop-only props each page lists. Nothing is mapped between the two APIs, with one exception: `SheetDrawer` passes the Sheet's `side` to the [drawer edge](/docs/sheet-drawer#the-drawer-edge). A `PopoverDrawer` therefore opens its drawer from the bottom, wherever the Popover is placed.

## Shared root

`drawer` on the root leaves out `RootState`, the keys that belong to the shared root: `open` and `defaultOpen`, since both sides share one open state, and the detached-trigger props `handle`, `triggerId` and `defaultTriggerId`. `drawer` on the Trigger leaves out `handle` and `payload`.

Detached triggers are desktop-only on Base UI. Below 768px the drawer opens only from a Trigger placed inside the root, or from a controlled `open`.
